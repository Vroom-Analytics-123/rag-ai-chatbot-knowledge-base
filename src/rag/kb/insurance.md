# Insurance & Payment FAQs

We accept Delta Dental PPO, Cigna, and Aetna plans. Bring your insurance card to every visit so we can verify coverage before treatment.

Self-pay patients receive a 10% discount when paying in full at the time of the visit. We accept cash, credit cards, and HSA or FSA cards.

Payment is due at the time of your appointment unless a payment plan was arranged in advance. Estimates are provided before any major treatment begins.

If your plan requires a referral or pre-authorization, please arrange it before your visit — we cannot backdate authorizations once treatment is complete.
