# Booking Rules

Appointments can be booked by phone at (555) 014-2288 or through the online booking page on our website. Both reach the same schedule.

New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake forms. Returning patients should arrive 5 minutes before their slot.

A standard cleaning appointment is 60 minutes. Exams with X-rays are 45 minutes, and most filling appointments are 90 minutes.

You will receive a confirmation text message the day before your appointment. Reply YES to confirm, or call us to make changes.
