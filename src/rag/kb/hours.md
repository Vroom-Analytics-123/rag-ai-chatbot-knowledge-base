# Hours & Holidays

Bright Smile Dental is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday hours are 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The office is closed on Sundays.

The last appointment of each day is scheduled 45 minutes before closing time, so same-day availability depends on the hour you call.

We are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. If a holiday falls on a Saturday, we close the Friday before instead.

Evening hours are not currently offered. If you need an early-morning slot, call as soon as we open — those go first.
