# Cancellations & No-Shows

We require 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. You can cancel by phone or by replying to your confirmation text.

A no-show fee of $50 applies when an appointment is missed without any notice. The fee is added to your account and must be paid before your next visit.

Patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled, and the visit may count as a no-show if the schedule cannot absorb the delay.

Repeated no-shows — three or more in twelve months — may require a deposit to book future appointments.
